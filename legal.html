Plans and payments

Free is free for good. Creator (€4.99 a month or €49 a year, 10 renders a month), Pro (€9.99 or €99, 50 renders) and Studio (€24.99 or €249, 250 renders) are subscriptions you choose in the app (Your account → Plan); prices include VAT where it applies. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or keep your card details. A subscription renews by itself at the end of each period until you cancel it; you can change or cancel it anytime under Manage subscription, and it stays active until the end of the period you paid for. A render is one exported video file; a render that is stopped or fails is given back, and renders start again on the 1st of every month. The plans also unlock Remix, Rotation and the studio's finer controls, tagged CREATOR+ in the app. Lyrics from the song need an account: a free one includes 10 minutes a month, the plans have no limit. If you live in the EU and change your mind, write to contact@demrender.com within 14 days of your first payment and we refund it.

Privacy

Your work. Your songs, pictures and projects are read from and saved to your own computer only — even the lyrics of a song are worked out on your computer. DemRender has no analytics and no tracking.

Your account. To keep your plan, DemRender has accounts. We keep: your email address and name; your password only as a one-way hash of a hash (the password itself never leaves your computer), or your Google account's ID if you sign in with Google; the computers you sign in on (a name such as "MY-PC · Windows", when it was last used, its IP address and country); how many renders and minutes of lyrics you used this month; and, if you subscribe, your Stripe customer number and plan. We use them only to run your account and plan, and to keep it safe. Security logs (sign-ins and failed attempts, with the IP address) are deleted after 90 days.

Who helps us. Cloudflare (runs our server and database, in the EU and elsewhere), Google (only if you sign in with Google), Brevo (sends the codes to your email) and Stripe (payments). Each handles your data only to provide that service.

Your rights. You can see your data in the app (Your account), sign computers out, and delete your account there at any time (Security → Delete): it is erased at once, apart from the security logs (gone within 90 days) and what the law requires us to keep about payments. For a copy of your data or any question, write to contact@demrender.com.

Updates. To find updates, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version has been published — GitHub sees that request like any visit to a web page (your IP address), and nothing else is sent.

This website. demrender.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies and no third-party scripts. To show the newest version on the Download button, your browser asks GitHub's public API for the latest release; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that and the hosting. To know how many people visit, each page sends DemRender's own server one anonymous count: the page, the name of the site you came from, whether it is a phone or a computer, and the country worked out from the connection — and a Download click sends one more. Your IP address is not stored, nothing is saved in your browser, and nothing identifies you: only daily totals are kept. If your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), it is not counted at all.

Email. If you write to contact@demrender.com, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Open-source notices

DemRender is not open source, but it includes open-source components, each under its own licence:

  • FFmpeg — writes the video files. GNU Lesser General Public License v3. DemRender runs it as a separate program (resources\ffmpeg in the Windows installation, resources/ffmpeg inside the Linux AppImage), which you may replace with your own build. Its complete source code and build scripts are published with every release on GitHub (see SOURCES.txt); for three years from each release we also send the source of any library compiled into it on request, at contact@demrender.com.
  • Electron and Chromium — the app runtime. MIT and the Chromium licences (LICENSES.chromium.html in the installation).
  • Fonts — Inter, Manrope, Archivo, JetBrains Mono, Playfair Display, Bebas Neue. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  • electron-updater and its dependencies — MIT / ISC.

The full texts are in resources\licenses in your DemRender folder (inside the AppImage on Linux), and in the app under About → Licences.
