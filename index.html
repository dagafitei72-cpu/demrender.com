Real 3D scenes — liquid chrome, gem crystals, a city at night, a valley made of your song — effects that erupt out of your cover, neon halo shapes, spectrum blobs in the style of the big music channels, line-art worlds and cinematic scenes, all rendered on the graphics card. The creator

A full studio underneath.

Every visualizer is built from layers you can open and change: GPU scenes, spectrum and waveforms, particles, shapes, your cover and animated text. Make any setting follow the bass, the beat or the vocals — or draw it on the timeline.

  • Colours taken from your cover in one click
  • Edit the song: trim it, fade in and out, louder or softer, a five-band equaliser — the video gets it too
  • Loop it and slow it down to check every beat
  • Effects on the beat: zoom, shake, RGB split, glitch, ripple
  • Animated titles and lyrics you sync by tapping along
  • An image catalog of original covers and backdrops, free to publish
  • Full undo history, autosave and crash recovery

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Is DemRender really free?

Yes. The whole app — every visualizer, the creator and export up to 4K — is free. We may add an optional Pro edition later; what is free today stays free.

Is there a watermark?

No. Your videos come out exactly as you made them.

Can I monetise the videos I make?

Yes. The videos are yours, and DemRender's visualizers and catalog pictures are free to use in them, including on monetised channels. You need the rights to the music and pictures you bring yourself — your own tracks, or ones you have a licence for.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a 64-bit Linux from the last few years (for example Ubuntu 22.04, Linux Mint 21 or newer); 8 GB of memory and a graphics card with up-to-date drivers. For 4K and 60 fps a dedicated graphics card is recommended. About 450 MB of disk space.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC” when I run the installer.

DemRender is new and its installer does not carry a code-signing certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about it. Click More info and then Run anyway. Download DemRender only from this page or from our GitHub releases.

Does it run on Linux? And on Mac?

Yes, on Linux: download the AppImage, allow it to run (right-click → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing file as program, or chmod +x DemRender-*.AppImage) and open it — nothing to install, and it updates itself just like the Windows version. If nothing happens when you open it, your system may be missing FUSE 2 support: install the package usually called libfuse2. A Mac version is not available yet.

How do updates work?

DemRender looks for a new version when it starts and downloads it in the background, on Windows and on Linux. When it's ready, a Restart to update button appears — or it simply installs the next time you close the app.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your computer: your songs, pictures and projects never leave it. The only thing DemRender asks the internet is whether a new version exists.

Questions, ideas, a bug?

Write to us — we read everything, and ideas for new visualizers are very welcome.
